    Timeline from junior year to applications

    Outlined dates unlock scholarships or the strongest admission round. Early decision is binding, so apply ED to one school at most.

    1. Spring 2027SAT or ACT. Yale, Johns Hopkins and Cornell require scores; Howard's BS/MD requires 1300+ SAT or 28+ ACT; Hampton merit aid requires scores.
    2. Summer 2027Clinical exposure (hospital volunteering, shadowing) and research. Draft the Common App essay.
    3. Early fall 2027Ask the counselor about nominations: Morehead-Cain (UNC), Jefferson and Walentas (UVA). Choose one binding early decision school, if any.
    4. Oct 15, 2027UNC-Chapel Hill early action (Morehead-Cain, Chancellor's Science Scholars).
    5. ~Nov 1, 2027Early round for Yale, Duke, Johns Hopkins, Cornell, Emory, UVA and Howard. Howard merit and FAFSA priority; Karsh STEM Scholars opens.
    6. Nov 15, 2027Hampton early action and merit; Emory Scholar Programs; Robertson (UNC/Duke).
    7. Dec 1, 2027Jefferson nomination materials; UNC aid forms.
    8. Jan 1–15, 2028Regular decision: Emory Jan 1; Yale, Johns Hopkins, Cornell Jan 2; Duke ~Jan 2–4; UVA Jan 5; UNC Jan 15. Karsh closes Jan 31.
    9. Feb 1–Mar 1, 2028Howard regular decision Feb 1; Hampton regular decision Mar 1.
